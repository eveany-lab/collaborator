# Contributing to Eveany Projects

## Our Philosophy: The Prompt is the DNA
In this project, we prioritize the **Authenticity of the Source**. We recognize that AI-generated code is a direct coefficient of the human intent expressed through **Input Prompting Tokens**.

### 1. Contribution Requirements
* **Input-First:** When submitting code, you are certifying that the core logic and architectural intent are derived from your original prompts.
* **Custodian Status:** You are the sole human custodian of the output. The AI is treated as a compiler of your specific input.
* **Platform Agnosticism:** Contributions must be tool-agnostic. We care about the result and the prompt, not which LLM or IDE was used.

### 2. The Submission Process
1. **Commit Attribution:** Every commit must credit the human author. 
2. **Prompt Documentation:** For complex logic, we encourage (but do not mandate) the inclusion of the 'Base Prompt' in the commit description to preserve the "Input DNA."
3. **Licensing:** By contributing, you agree to the terms of the **Eveany Input-Coefficient License (EICL)**.
