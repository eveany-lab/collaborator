# Eveany Input-Coefficient License (EICL)
**Scope: Universal, Platform-Agnostic, and Input-Driven.**

## I. Definitions

* **"Input Tokens"**: The specific sequence of characters, instructions, and context provided by a human ("The Author") to an automated system.
* **"Output Coefficient"**: The resulting digital artifact (code, text, or logic) generated as a direct response to the Input Tokens.
* **"The Custodian"**: The human author who maintains intellectual control and legal responsibility over the Output Coefficient.

## II. Grant of Rights

* **Indistinguishable Attribution**: For the purposes of this license, the Output Coefficient and Input Tokens are considered a singular intellectual entity. The Author of the Input is the de jure Author of the Output.
* **Custodian Ownership**: Full Intellectual Property (IP) rights and copyright (where applicable by law) are vested in the human Custodian, prioritizing the creative step of "Prompting" as the primary act of authorship.
* **Platform Neutrality**: These rights are independent of the platform, model, or tool used to process the Input Tokens.

## III. Obligations

* **Integrity of Input**: The Custodian must ensure the Input Tokens do not infringe on existing third-party copyrights or licenses.
* **Responsibility**: As the owner of the "Coefficient," the Custodian assumes all liability for the functional and ethical outcomes of the code.

## IV. Distribution

Any software distributed under the EICL must maintain the "Input-Output Unity" in its metadata, ensuring the human prompter is never decoupled from their generated work.
